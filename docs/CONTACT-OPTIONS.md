## Interaction

1. On a human-review answer, choose Email or Call; neither button navigates.
2. Read the real-contact warning and contact preview.
3. An additional explicit link opens the native app if supported:
   - `tel:` opens the device phone handler. OS/browser behavior varies; the site does not make or schedule calls. Mobile call charges may apply.
   - `mailto:` opens the mail handler with a clearly marked fictional demo draft. It does not send mail automatically. No transcript or raw user input is included; only a category summary generated by the app.
4. Back removes the pending link. A new handoff mounts a fresh selection. Missing/invalid contacts open an explanation and the verified contact-page fallback, without a phone/email URI.

For testing, stop at the preview or close the native draft without sending; do not confirm calls. The demo does not have a chat agent, queue, inbox, callback service, ticket delivery or response-time commitment. On desktop, opening these URI handlers requires a configured compatible app.

## Production reuse

Replace tenant public contacts only with approved company channels and review the explanatory text. To offer real chat, implement and agree a staffed inbox integration first. Do not reuse the fictional demo email draft as a production ticket workflow.
