## Defects and fixes

1. case-07 RU: routine change-of-mind question returned the right policy but added unnecessary human review.
2. case-08 LV: same unnecessary human review, plus an unasked refund paragraph.
3. case-45 EN: correct defect card plus an irrelevant withdrawal-refund card. The latter can confuse separate customer remedies and must be removed unless money-back/withdrawal was actually requested.

Fixes tighten classifier instructions, remove unsolicited refund cards when alongside return/defect guidance, and clear model-added handoff only in a bounded change-of-mind case without detected complications or prior sensitive context. Numbers, dates/durations, product damage, opened/hygiene goods, business purchase, explicit staff requests and disputes preserve review. Existing mandatory-review cards remain mandatory. The bounded correction is not a general semantic guarantee.
