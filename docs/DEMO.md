# Demonstration script (2–3 minutes)

Use `Anete-demo-offline.html` for a free portable reference demonstration, or the deployed Worker after authorised configuration. State the mode before starting. The portable page uses the same approved cards and reference router, but has no OpenAI connection.

1. Show the independent-demo label and explain the scope: public policies, three languages, no customer records.
2. RU: «Какие способы доставки есть?» Show the answer and clickable policy source.
3. RU: «Гарантируете завтра?» Show that an exact date is not promised.
4. Switch to LV (this starts a fresh conversation): «Kā apmaksāt pasūtījumu?»
5. LV: «Kāda garantija uzņēmumam?» Show that conflicting information is referred for human verification.
6. RU: «Отмените заказ». Show that the assistant does not claim to have cancelled anything; open/copy the handoff category summary. Nothing is sent.
7. Switch to EN: “How do I return an item?” Show the English answer and its policy source.
8. Explain the difference between tested reference behavior and live AI understanding. Do not claim a percentage of support tickets resolved or financial savings without real measurements.

Before pitching to Anete.lv, obtain the user's explicit permission to contact them. Before a commercial pilot, confirm policies and escalation ownership with the shop, check Latvian wording, configure the real support inbox, and run the live evaluation suite. This demo must not be installed on their site without their permission.

Contact options: ask to contact a human, choose email/phone, inspect the preview and press Back. Do not activate the native-app link during a sales demonstration unless the viewer intentionally wants real contact. Phone/email are real shop contacts; no real staff chat is connected.

Acceptance checkpoint (15 October 2026): 50 baseline production cases reviewed; three identified defects fixed and confirmed by a targeted 3/3 live rerun. Demonstration readiness is limited to approved policy guidance, RU/LV/EN, courtesy handling and transparent contact options. Do not claim order access, a staffed live chat, a full 50-case final-build rerun or universal accuracy.
